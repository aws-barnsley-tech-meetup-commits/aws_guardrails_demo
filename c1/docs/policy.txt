QuickCart delivery, returns and payment policy.

Delivery
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 working days.
Express delivery arrives the next working day if the order is placed before 8pm.
The express upgrade costs GBP 5.99.

Returns
Returns are accepted within 28 days of delivery.
The item must be unused and in its original packaging.

Refunds
The refund covers the item price only.
The original delivery charge is not refunded.
If the item is faulty, the original delivery charge is refunded as well, and QuickCart covers the return postage.
If the item is not faulty, the customer pays the return postage.
The GBP 5.99 express upgrade is refunded only if the parcel arrived after the promised day.

Promotions
WELCOME10 gives 10% off a first order. It is the only promotion currently running.

Pay Later
QuickCart Pay Later spreads an order over 3 monthly payments at 0% interest, subject to eligibility.

Order status codes
PRE means preparing. DSP means dispatched. OFD means out for delivery. DLV means delivered. RTN means returned.

Contact
Customers who need something outside this policy are directed to support@quickcart.example.
